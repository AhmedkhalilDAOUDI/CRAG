      How an answer is made

      1. Understand. The question is classified as a lookup, an expert search or a recommendation, and its key names are picked out.
      2. Retrieve. Passages are found by meaning (and by keywords if you choose), and the graph is walked two relations deep.
      3. Check the evidence. If it is thin, the question is rewritten and retrieval runs once more.
      4. Answer. The model writes short claims, each with the passages and facts it relies on.
      5. Verify. Every quoted phrase must appear word for word in its passage. Claims that fail are removed.
